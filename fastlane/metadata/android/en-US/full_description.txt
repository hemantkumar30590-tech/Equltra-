Equltra is an AMOLED black Material 3 10-Band System-wide Equalizer, Bass Booster, Spatial 3D, and Acoustic Reverb DSP engine for Android.

Features:
- 10-Band Graphic & Parametric Equalizer (31Hz to 16kHz, -15dB to +15dB)
- YouTube & System-wide Audio Hooking via Android DynamicsProcessing and LoudnessEnhancer
- Sub-Harmonic Bass Boost, Binaural Spatial 3D, Schroeder Reverb, and Preamp with 64-Bit Soft-Clip Limiter
- 15 Device-Specific EQ Presets (AirPods Pro 3, AirPods 4 ANC, Sony WF-1000XM5/XM6, WH-1000XM6, Galaxy Buds 3 Pro / 4 Pro, Bose QC Ultra 2nd Gen, Sennheiser Momentum 4, Pixel Buds Pro 2, JBL Tour Pro 3, OnePlus Buds Pro 3, Nothing Ear (a), Boat/Noise/Realme Buds, Over-ear Generic)
- 9 Studio Mastering Presets (Flat, Bass Boost, Warm, Theater, Dialogue Boost, Action Mode, Soft, Late Night, Vocal Clarity)
- Custom Preset Import/Export (.json, AutoEQ .txt, .equltra) with WhatsApp/Telegram sharing
- Persistent Equltra Background DSP ACTIVE notification service
